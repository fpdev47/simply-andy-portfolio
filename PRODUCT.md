# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Brand managers and marketing leads at Spanish-speaking (local and international) consumer brands, plus global brands wanting authentic Spanish-language UGC. They arrive from a pitch email, a TikTok/Instagram bio link, or a creator marketplace, usually skimming on a laptop or phone between other candidate reviews. Their job: decide in under a minute whether Andy's voice and content style fit their product, and find how to contact her.

## Product Purpose

A one-page portfolio for Simply Andy (@__simplyandy), a 35-year-old UGC creator making Spanish-language content for TikTok and Instagram Reels across lifestyle, wellness, beauty, tech, and food. The site must convey her differentiator, content that feels like a trusted friend's recommendation rather than an ad, and convert brand visitors into email contact. Success: a brand skims the page, feels her voice, and writes to itssimplyandy1@gmail.com. Bilingual ES/EN (ES is the primary voice; the copy uses Rioplatense voseo).

## Brand Personality

Cercana, auténtica, cálida. The site should feel like Andy talking: direct, no fluff, first-person, lightly playful. Warm and handcrafted rather than corporate; confident without selling hard. Emotional goal: trust, the feeling of a friend telling you about something that actually worked.

## Anti-references

- Aggressive advertising energy: artificial urgency, loud promo banners, infomercial tone, countdowns, "hire me now" pressure. The whole brand premise is "doesn't feel like an ad"; the site must practice that.
- Generic agency-slick corporate sites and cookie-cutter portfolio templates.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice the premise.** The pitch is "content that doesn't feel like an ad", so the site itself must never feel like one. Persuade through warmth and specificity, not pressure.
2. **First-person voice everywhere.** Copy reads like Andy speaking, in both languages; UI text stays conversational, never corporate.
3. **Warm and handcrafted.** Cream/caramel/deep-purple palette, soft radii, ambient motion. Playful details (floating dots, tilted cards) over rigid grids.
4. **Honest placeholders.** Content still pending (photos, videos, brands, stats) is labeled as coming, never faked as real.
5. **Motion as garnish, progressive enhancement.** Animations run on top of a fully usable static page: reduced-motion, no-JS, and slow connections all get the complete content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target. Already in place and to be preserved: `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation, aria-labels on interactive/decorative-stat elements, focus-visible styles, semantic landmarks, bilingual `lang` handling. Body text contrast ≥4.5:1 including on the dark contact section.
